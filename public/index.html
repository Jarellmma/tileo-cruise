Controls

Keyboard: WASD / arrows move · Shift hold or R toggle sprint · Space jump · drag mouse to look · wheel zoom · E interact · G dance · Q emote wheel (1-8 pick) · V 1st/3rd person · K mini-map · Tab full map · P phone menu · N day/sunset/night · M music · Esc close

Phone: left joystick moves (push to the edge to run) · drag the screen to look · right thumb: ⤒ jump, 🏃 sprint, 😀 emotes, 💃 dance, ✋ interact · top: 📱 menu, 🗺️ mini-map (tap the map for the full Strip), 🎥 camera

Photo mode: C or 📸 anywhere · Space shutter · F rear/selfie · 1-4 filters (none, warm, B&W, neon) · W watermark · G gallery · drag to aim, wheel/pinch to zoom.

Airport: you land at Sin City International baggage claim. Grab your bag off Carousel 11 (walk up to it), stock the suite at the Booze Library, then take your limo from the pickup curb up the Strip to TILEO KINGDOM and check in with the concierge at the front desk: the bellman takes your bags up. ⏭ on the checklist skips to the lobby. Drinks you buy wait on your suite's bar cart. Teleport ✈️ to come back.

Limo: hail at the TILEO KINGDOM curb, from 📱 Limo, or teleport. V camera view · Space stand in the sunroof · B radio · O windows · 📍 pick a stop · X end ride.

Sky Needle: ride the street-level elevator next to the tower or teleport to the top. The deck has an indoor and an outdoor ring; walk all the way around. 🔭 binoculars cost 5 coins.

Driving: W/S gas & brake/reverse, A/D steer, H or 📯 horn, L or 💡 headlights, B or 📻 radio station, E exit car.

Music: 📱 → Music shows what's playing: skip songs, change the car, limo or suite station, or request a song from DJ Tileo in the Mega Club.

Casino tables: walk up to the roulette wheel or a blackjack table and press E. Blackjack pays 3:2, dealer stands on 17; roulette has a single zero. Play coins only.

Online (website): Y or 👥 shows who is here, chat and parties. Public Strips use preset phrases; start a private party and send the invite link to chat freely. 👑 Account in the phone signs in with your TILEO account (the same one as TILEO Cruise Lines).
